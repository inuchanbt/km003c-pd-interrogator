# Hardware verification, 2026-11-05 and 2026-11-06

English summary · [日本語の詳細記録](HARDWARE-2026-11-05.ja.md)

Testing used one exact-build HW 2.3 / stock 2.0.7 meter. The [full Japanese log](HARDWARE-2026-11-05.ja.md) records the individual builds, private evidence paths, and measured states.

- **Install and recover.** The dedicated updater installed and started the overlay. Readback matched the expected internal ranges and loader, and the normal application reported the expected calibration bytes. Two-stage restoration returned the internal regions, loader, and calibration readback to the original backup. The stock screen and buttons resumed normal operation.
- **QR screen.** Device-generated pages imported on iPhone. Closing and reopening the QR screen preserved the completed result and measurement ID.
- **Larger page labels.** The 10-pixel label build passed installation readback and normal startup. The owner confirmed that the labels were easier to read on the meter and that the displayed QR codes scanned successfully.
- **Source.** The reference EPR source returned SPR/EPR lists and six additional query results. The measured source reported PD revision 3.2, specification version 1.1, VID 32AC, PID 0022, SPR PDP 100 W and EPR PDP 240 W. The source's reported PDP field was 1 W and was preserved. Delayed EPR PDOs were included after waiting for the current detection, and the final set imported on iPhone.
- **Cable.** A standalone e-marker result imported in two pages. A subsequent EJ903W test with zero Identity words exported SVIDs `8087 / 1E4E` as a partial result. The two pages opened directly from the stock result screen.
- **Manual PPS.** The stock SPR trigger requested 5.10 V and 5.00 A. The source PPS Status reported 5.08 V and 0 A at no load, constant-voltage operation, and normal temperature. Both QR pages imported on iPhone.

These observations describe the tested unit, cables, and supplies. Offline emulator cases are recorded with their respective subsystem. For installation and recovery, see the [Windows guide](INSTALL.md).
